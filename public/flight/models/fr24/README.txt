Aircraft exterior models from the Flightradar24 3D models repository
https://github.com/Flightradar24/fr24-3d-models  (source files: /source in that repository)
Original models by the FlightGear project and FGMEMBERS contributors (https://github.com/FGMEMBERS).
License: GNU General Public License v2 (see LICENSE in this folder).
Modifications in this game: converted from glTF 1.0 to glTF 2.0 with gltf-pipeline, then
re-encoded with gltf-transform (meshopt geometry compression, WebP textures at 1024 px).
No geometry was otherwise changed. Aircraft type names are used descriptively; this game is
not affiliated with or endorsed by Airbus, Boeing, Bombardier, Embraer, ATR, De Havilland,
Cessna, Piper, Schleicher or Flightradar24.
